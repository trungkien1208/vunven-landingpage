## FAQ (credits 2)
kicker: Behind the scenes
title: Things people ask
lead: Anything else? Write to us at support@tdk.best
Q1 What do I need to log every day? / Whatever you want to keep track of: an expense, how you feel today, a health reading. You don't need to fill in everything.
Q2 Do I have to connect my bank? / No. Type it in, or paste a bank text to fill it in faster. Vun Vén never connects to your account and never asks for passwords or OTPs.
Q3 Can my whole family see what I log? / Your journal and feelings are private by default. For spending, you choose shared or personal. For health, you choose who can see each record.
Q4 Can I log for parents who live far away? / Yes. Make a profile for them to track readings, medicines and check-ups. They don't need to install the app.
Q5 Is it really free forever? / Yes. Logging, reminders and family alerts are always free. Hun Plus only adds reports, PDFs and higher limits.
Q6 How many Hun Plus plans does my family need? / One. When one person subscribes, the whole family gets it.
